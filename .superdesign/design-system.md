# LAB8 Kinetic Blue Design System

## Product context

LAB8 is a Brazilian Social Media Design studio. It transforms visually weak, inconsistent Instagram profiles into professional, strategic, organized brands ready to sell.

- Brand: LAB8
- Descriptor: Social Media Design
- Instagram: `@LAB8.md`
- Core promise: transform weak profiles into professional, strategic and commercially ready brands.
- Primary conversion: WhatsApp quote/contact.
- Secondary conversion: portfolio and package exploration.
- Audience: professionals, creators, service businesses and small brands that need credible social positioning.

## Governing style source

Create from the extracted design DNA at:

- `.superdesign/website/kinetic-orange-style/design.md`
- `.superdesign/website/kinetic-orange-style/content-structure.md`
- `.superdesign/website/kinetic-orange-style/tokens.json`

Adopt its brutalist, editorial, asymmetric, high-contrast and kinetic visual language faithfully, with one governing substitution:

- Kinetic Orange `#FF4D00` becomes LAB8 Royal Blue `#2D4BFF`.

The result is **Kinetic Blue**: royal blue, black and white; oversized typography; sharp geometry; asymmetric off-edge composition; hard dividers; dark navigation pills; marquees, rotating circular cues and brisk directional motion.

### Color rules

- Royal blue should own approximately 40–50% of the hero and appear in decisive full fields, not small random accents.
- Black supplies 15–25% through typography, navigation, dividers and dark sections.
- White/off-white supplies breathing room and secondary editorial sections.
- No gold.
- No gradients, including blue-to-black or blue-to-white gradients.
- No transparent glow, glassmorphism, neon aura or blurred color atmosphere.
- Borders are solid black or solid white, never subtle translucent gray.

## Typography

### Primary display

- Family: Montserrat
- Weights: 700 Bold and 600 Semibold only for titles
- Hero: oversized, compressed through tight leading; desktop target 96–168px depending on composition
- Section display: 64–112px desktop
- Tracking: `-0.04em` to `-0.075em`
- Line-height: `0.82–0.95`
- Use sentence fragments and deliberate line breaks.

### Secondary text

- Family: Poppins
- Weights: 400 Regular and 500 Medium
- Body: 16–20px, line-height `1.45–1.65`
- UI labels: 11–13px, uppercase, medium, tracking `0.08em–0.16em`
- Captions/metadata: 11–14px

### Hierarchy

1. Display headlines: Montserrat Bold, monumental and editorial.
2. Section headlines: Montserrat Semibold/Bold.
3. Navigation, eyebrow and technical labels: Poppins Medium uppercase.
4. Body and explanations: Poppins Regular.
5. Numeric indices: Montserrat Semibold or Poppins Medium, large enough to become graphic anchors.

Never introduce serif, script, handwritten, futuristic sci-fi, crypto-style or decorative display fonts.

## Temporary logo system

The current repository SVGs are visually rejected for the redesign.

- Do not reuse, trace, repair, reinterpret or auto-correct `lab8-logo.svg`, `lab8-logo-light.svg` or `lab8-icon.svg` in the redesign or Brand Kit.
- Use a carefully typeset temporary wordmark: `LAB8` in Montserrat Bold, tight tracking, optically balanced.
- Horizontal lockup: `LAB8` + small `SOCIAL MEDIA DESIGN`.
- Vertical lockup: wordmark above descriptor.
- Temporary symbol: geometric `8` built from two opposing hard-edged loops or intersecting bands; it must read as a design-system placeholder, not as a final official vector.
- Reserve stable header, footer, favicon/avatar and presentation-signature zones for later replacement by official vector assets.
- Label the Brand Kit logo work as a **temporary typographic construction pending official vector artwork**.

## Composition

- Full-width, edge-to-edge sections.
- Asymmetric editorial grid with deliberate off-edge crops.
- 12-column desktop grid; 4-column mobile grid.
- Hard horizontal and vertical dividers.
- Large fields of solid Royal Blue, Black and White.
- Use circles, arrows, numerals, cropped words, stripes, checker systems, scanning lines and oversized outlined geometry as kinetic graphic devices.
- Employ modular bands, split screens, staggered cards and cropped typography.
- Prefer square corners. Maximum ordinary radius: 8px. Pills are reserved for navigation, tags and compact CTAs.
- No soft drop shadows; use contrast, offset duplicate blocks or 1–2px hard lines.
- Do not constrain every section to one centered max-width container.

## Motion

- Marquee strips: horizontal service, benefit or manifesto phrases.
- Rotating circular text or rotating directional cue near the hero/section transitions.
- Fast 0.2–0.3s cubic-bezier interactions.
- Directional arrow movement on CTA hover.
- Hard wipe/reveal using blue, black or white panels.
- Number counters and staggered section indices where meaningful.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid floating particles, slow parallax fog, blob morphs or ambient AI animation.

## Iconography

- Simple line icons with consistent 1.5–2px stroke.
- Geometric arrows, plus signs, corner marks, grid coordinates and editorial symbols.
- Default black on white, white on black, black on blue.
- No 3D icons, glossy app icons, emoji illustration or mixed icon styles.

## Photography and mockup direction

- Documentary, creative-process and screen-in-use imagery with candid framing.
- Strong crops, monochrome or natural photography combined with solid blue/black graphic overlays.
- Show real social feed transformations, layout systems, grid logic and working files.
- Digital mockups should be flat, credible and interface-led.
- No fake luxury stationery, marble desks, gold foil, floating phones, glossy product pedestals or synthetic premium-office scenes.
- When real portfolio assets are unavailable, use honest labeled placeholders, graphic feed compositions or wireframe-like proof panels rather than invented client results.

## Voice and copy

Tone: direct, confident, strategic, creative and commercially aware.

- Short declarative headlines.
- Avoid generic AI claims: “revolutionary”, “unlock your potential”, “next level”, “seamless”, “future-proof”.
- Speak in concrete transformations: weak → professional, scattered → organized, improvised → strategic, profile → brand.
- Use editorial fragments and contrasts.
- CTA language should be specific: “Quero transformar meu perfil”, “Ver transformações”, “Escolher meu pacote”.

Example headlines:

- `SEU PERFIL NÃO PRECISA PARECER IMPROVISADO.`
- `DE PERFIL FRACO A MARCA PRONTA PARA VENDER.`
- `DESIGN QUE ORGANIZA. ESTRATÉGIA QUE POSICIONA.`
- `NÃO É SÓ FEED BONITO. É SISTEMA DE MARCA.`
- `A LAB8 TRANSFORMA PRESENÇA EM PERCEPÇÃO.`

## Homepage architecture

The redesign must contain all twelve sections below in this order and must feel like one coherent editorial narrative.

1. **Header** — temporary LAB8 wordmark, compact dark pill navigation, strong quote CTA.
2. **Hero** — full-impact Kinetic Blue composition, monumental headline, asymmetric off-edge text, kinetic circular scroll cue, solid fields only.
3. **Manifesto** — why weak profiles lose value; bold editorial statement and marquee.
4. **Services** — cover all services: Instagram restructuring, visual identity, logo creation/redesign, brand book, Canva templates, social direction, bio/positioning, content planning, monthly kits.
5. **Before/after transformation** — honest split comparison with “ANTES” and “DEPOIS”, showing organization, clarity, visual consistency and sales readiness.
6. **Method** — diagnosis, positioning, visual direction, system creation, editable delivery and guidance.
7. **Portfolio** — bold project index/cards; use labeled design placeholders if real assets are absent.
8. **Packages** — three readable tiers adapted from current content, with a high-contrast featured package.
9. **Benefits** — professional perception, consistency, speed, strategic clarity, editability and sales readiness.
10. **FAQ** — direct accordion treatment with hard dividers.
11. **Final CTA** — full Royal Blue field, monumental conversion headline and WhatsApp CTA.
12. **Footer** — temporary wordmark, descriptor, `@LAB8.md`, navigation and replacement-ready official-logo zone.

## Anti-slop guardrails

- No generic startup hero with centered headline and floating dashboard card.
- No generic SaaS cards on a pastel background.
- No cryptocurrency cues, token charts, coins, chains or Web3 motifs.
- No gold, glassmorphism, neon, holographic surfaces or generic gradients.
- No artificial luxury mockups.
- No traditional corporate blue template.
- No random blobs, sparkles, abstract 3D shapes or stock “AI brain” imagery.
- No unearned fake case-study metrics.
- Every visual device must originate in the Kinetic reference DNA or LAB8 positioning.
- Use ONLY Montserrat, Poppins, the defined palette, spacing and component styles. Do not introduce any other fonts, colors or visual styles.
